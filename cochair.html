How It Works

The email is the trigger. The Action Card is the answer.

CoChair works entirely within the context of the email in front of you — no separate workflow, no data entry, no switching tabs.

1

An email request arrives

A student emails asking for a capacity permit. A faculty member requests a prerequisite override. A registrar flags a room conflict. CoChair reads the email and identifies what's being asked and who's asking.

2

CoChair pulls the context from your systems

Using the student name or ID from the email, CoChair queries Banner for their record — GPA, completed prerequisites, enrollment status — and Workday for course availability. All from a single email.

3

Action Card appears in your Gmail sidebar

A structured card surfaces alongside the email — showing the data CoChair found, any policy flags, open section alternatives, and a recommendation. Everything in context, right next to the message.

4

One click closes the loop

Approve, deny, schedule, or refer. CoChair drafts the reply in the context of the original email, sends it, and records the decision — without you leaving Gmail.

Compliance & Security

Built for higher education data standards

CoChair was designed from the ground up for the compliance requirements of higher education institutions.

✓

FERPA Compliant

Student education records never leave your institution's systems. CoChair queries your SIS using your own credentials — data is displayed, not stored.

✓

No Student Data Retained

CoChair does not persist student records, transcripts, or personally identifiable information on Cephgate servers. Each query is ephemeral.

✓

Role-Based Access

Only authorized users see Action Cards. Access is controlled by your institution's existing authentication and role configuration in Banner or Workday.

✓

Data Processing Agreement

Cephgate signs a DPA with each institution as a FERPA "school official" — the same vendor agreement model used by Ellucian, Workday, and other SIS partners.

✓

Encrypted in Transit

All communication between CoChair and your systems uses TLS 1.2+ encryption. API credentials are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest.

✓

Audit Trail

Every decision made through CoChair is logged with timestamp, user, and action taken — supporting your institution's records retention requirements.
