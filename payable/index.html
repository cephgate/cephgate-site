How Payable works

Three steps. No setup. No manual data entry.

1
📧

Open an invoice email

Open any email with an invoice or receipt attachment in Gmail. Payable activates automatically in the sidebar.

2
🤖

AI extracts everything

Payable reads the PDF, image, or Word doc — extracting vendor, amount, due date, PO number, and more. New vendors are created automatically.

3
📗

Save to QuickBooks

Click Save as Bill or Record Expense. Done. The transaction is in QuickBooks with the original document attached.

Everything you need for AP

Built for small teams who live in Gmail and QuickBooks.

📄

PDF, image & Word support

Payable extracts data from any attachment type — scanned PDFs, photographed receipts, emailed invoices as images or Word docs.

🏢

Auto vendor creation

New vendor? No problem. Payable extracts the contact info from the invoice and creates the vendor record in QuickBooks automatically.

📗

Bill or Expense routing

Invoices become Bills. Receipts become Expenses. Payable detects which is which and routes to the right QuickBooks transaction type.

🔍

Duplicate detection

Already paid this invoice? Payable checks QuickBooks before saving and warns you if the same invoice number already exists.

⚠️

Risk flagging

Payable flags potential issues — PO mismatches, new vendors, unusual amounts, and missing data — before you save.

🔗

Works where you work

No new app. No new login. Payable lives in your Gmail sidebar and connects directly to your existing QuickBooks Online account.
