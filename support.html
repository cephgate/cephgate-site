Payable Support

Get help setting up Payable, using the add-on, and connecting QuickBooks.

Getting Started

1

Install the Gmail Add-on

Visit the Google Workspace Marketplace and search for "Payable by Cephgate." Click Install and grant the requested permissions. Payable only accesses emails you actively open — it never scans your inbox.

2

Connect QuickBooks

Open Gmail and click the Payable icon in the right sidebar. On the home card, tap Connect QuickBooks → and follow the Intuit authorization flow. You'll be redirected back to Gmail when complete.

3

Open an Invoice Email

Open any email containing an invoice or receipt — PDF, image, or Word document attachment. Payable will automatically detect it and begin analysis. Results appear in the sidebar within 10–20 seconds.

4

Review and Save

Review the extracted vendor, amount, category, and date. Then tap Save as Bill or Record Expense to push it directly to QuickBooks. A confirmation with a link to the QB record appears when complete.

Using Payable

Supported Document Types

  • PDF invoices and receipts — vendor invoices, receipts, statements
  • Image attachments — JPG/PNG photos of physical receipts or scanned documents
  • Word documents (.docx) — invoices or receipts sent as Word files

What Payable Extracts

  • Vendor name, email, phone, and address
  • Invoice number or receipt number
  • Total amount (using the correct total, not subtotals or line item rates)
  • Invoice date and due date
  • Payment terms (e.g. Net 30)
  • Suggested expense category
  • Payment method and last 4 digits (for credit card receipts)

Bills vs. Expenses

Save as Bill — use for unpaid vendor invoices with a due date. Creates a Bill in QuickBooks Accounts Payable. Requires QuickBooks Essentials or higher.

Record Expense — use for receipts and already-paid charges. Creates an Expense in QuickBooks. Available on all QuickBooks plans including Simple Start.

Note: If your QuickBooks plan doesn't support Bills, Payable will automatically save as an Expense instead.

New Vendors

If a vendor isn't in your QuickBooks, Payable will offer to add them automatically using the contact information extracted from the document. Review the pre-filled details and tap Add Vendor to QuickBooks before saving the bill or expense.

Settings Guide

Opening Settings

From the Payable home card (visible when no email is open), tap the ⚙️ Settings button. Use the native back arrow in the sidebar header to return.

Approval Threshold

Set a dollar amount above which invoices are flagged for additional review before saving. Payable will show a warning flag on any invoice exceeding this amount. Set to "off" to allow all invoices to save directly.

Payment Method Dropdown

Toggle this on to show a dropdown of your QuickBooks payment methods when recording expenses. Tap ↻ Sync Payments & Methods to load your accounts from QuickBooks. If turned off, Payable uses the payment method detected from the document.

Payment Account Dropdown

Similar to payment methods — shows your QuickBooks bank/credit card accounts when recording expenses. Useful if you want to specify which account an expense is charged to.

Default Category

Set a fallback category used when Payable cannot determine the category from the document. Leave blank to always use AI suggestion.

License Key

Enter your license key here to activate a paid plan. Keys are emailed to you after purchase at cephgate.com/payable.

QuickBooks Connection

Shows whether QuickBooks is connected. Use Reconnect if you've changed your QuickBooks password or if the connection has expired. Use Disconnect to revoke Payable's access to your QuickBooks account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does analysis take 10–20 seconds?

Payable sends your document to an AI model for extraction. PDF and text documents analyze faster (3–8 seconds). Large image files (phone photos) may take up to 20 seconds.

Payable says "No invoice detected" on an email with an attachment.

Payable currently supports PDF, image (JPG/PNG), and Word (.docx) attachments. Unsupported formats (e.g. Excel, CSV, ZIP) will show "No invoice detected." Try forwarding the email with a PDF version of the document.

The amount or vendor name was extracted incorrectly.

AI extraction is highly accurate but not perfect, especially on low-resolution images or non-standard document layouts. Always review extracted data before saving to QuickBooks. You can edit any field in QuickBooks after saving.

I hit my monthly save limit.

Free accounts include 5 saves per month. Upgrade to Pro ($12/mo, 30 saves) or Business ($39/mo, 250 saves) at cephgate.com/payable.

QuickBooks shows "Connection expired" or "Reconnect required."

QuickBooks OAuth tokens expire after 100 days of inactivity or when you change your Intuit password. Open Payable Settings and tap Reconnect to re-authorize.

How do I delete my data?

Disconnect QuickBooks from Settings to revoke token access. To request deletion of all stored data (QB tokens, usage records), email privacy@cephgate.com.

Does Payable work on mobile Gmail?

Gmail add-ons are currently supported on Gmail in desktop web browsers only. Mobile Gmail (iOS/Android app) does not support add-ons.
